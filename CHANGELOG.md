## 0.1.0-beta.1 (2026-11-03)

### New

- Record levels and how long each took, achievements, the first kill of each boss, first visits to zones and dungeons, duels, battlegrounds, new pets, new mounts and deaths, each with a line in chat and most with a screenshot
- Open the journal with `/footsteps`: every entry, newest first, with more in its tooltip and a filter for which kinds are listed
- Click an entry in the journal to see all it holds, copy its screenshot's file name, add a note or hide it; hidden entries can be shown and restored, and nothing is ever erased
- Choose for each character what is recorded, what takes a screenshot and whether screenshots hide the interface, in the game's settings under AddOns, and open the journal from a minimap button or the game's addon menu
- Put a caption in the corner of each screenshot saying what happened, who, where and when
